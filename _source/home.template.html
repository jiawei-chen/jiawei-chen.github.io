I am a “Hundred Talent” Research Fellow at the ZLST Lab in the College of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang University. I received my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University (advised by Prof. Chun Chen) and worked as a postdoctoral researcher at USTC (advised by Prof. Xiangnan He). My research focuses on recommender systems, large language models, and AI agents. I have published over 80 papers in top conferences and journals, including ACL, WWW, SIGIR, KDD, TOIS, and WSDM. My recent awards include the Outstanding Paper Award at ACL 2026, the Best Paper Award at WSDM 2025, and the Best Paper Honourable Mention at SIGIR 2023.

Recent Interests

  • Generative Recommendation. Exploring novel model architectures, optimization algorithms, and scaling laws of generative recommendation.
  • Large Language Models (LLMs). Focusing on post-training algorithms (e.g., SFT and RL) and downstream applications.
  • Autonomous Agents. Investigating self-evolving techniques and continuous learning capabilities of AI agents.

Prospective students: I am looking for self-motivated master’s and Ph.D. students. If you are interested in these topics, please email me your CV.
